# Brag Plan: OmGold ERP — Video 03/09 · Stock

> Series "OmGold ERP — poori series" ka ek episode (9 videos, Hinglish). Poori series ka index: `../README.md`.

## What is this app?
OmGold ERP jewellery dukaan ka poora software hai — GST billing, tag-wala stock, gold loan (girvi), udhaar, schemes aur double-entry bahi-khata ek hi jagah, web + desktop + mobile par.

## The angle
Stock dukaan ki jaan hai. Ek piece ko add karne se lekar tally, transfer, hallmark aur melting tak — har screen par "kahan click, kya bharna" dikhaya gaya hai, aur wazan → purity → daam ka hisaab screen ke numbers se.
